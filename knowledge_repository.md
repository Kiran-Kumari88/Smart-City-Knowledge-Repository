# Smart City Management Knowledge Repository

## 1. Traffic Management

Traffic management includes information about roads, traffic
density and traffic signals.

### Knowledge
- Roads are monitored for traffic conditions.
- Traffic density can be High, Medium or Low.
- Alternate routes can be suggested during high traffic.

---

## 2. Public Transport

Public transport knowledge includes buses, routes and their
availability.

### Knowledge
- Buses are assigned to specific routes.
- Available buses can be assigned to routes.
- Unavailable buses cannot be assigned.

---

## 3. Waste Management

Waste management includes garbage bins, their fill levels and
collection status.

### Knowledge
- Garbage bins are monitored based on their fill level.
- Collection is required when a bin reaches the defined threshold.

---

## 4. Hospitals

Hospital knowledge includes hospital capacity, available beds
and emergency facilities.

### Knowledge
- Hospital capacity is monitored.
- Available beds are used for handling patients.
- Emergency facilities support emergency situations.

---

## 5. Emergency Services

Emergency services include ambulances, police units and fire
trucks.

### Knowledge
- Ambulances are used for medical emergencies.
- Police units handle law and safety-related emergencies.
- Fire trucks respond to fire emergencies.

---

## Purpose of Repository

This repository stores and organizes knowledge related to
Smart City Management in a structured and easily accessible
form.

The repository can be updated whenever new knowledge or
information is added to the Smart City Management system.
